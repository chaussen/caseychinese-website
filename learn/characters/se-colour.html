The story behind it字源小故事

Find 色 at the end of every colour word: 红色, 黄色, 蓝色 — it is 'colour' itself.

A common word常用词

彩cǎi色sè

colourful

Example sentence例句

彩cǎi虹hóng有yǒu七qī种zhǒng颜yán色sè。

A rainbow has seven colours.

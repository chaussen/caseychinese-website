看 kàn — “to look, to read”

How to write and say the Chinese character 看 (kàn), which means to look, to read — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Everyday actions.

A common word常用词

to see

Example sentence例句

看kàn，天tiān上shàng有yǒu一yì朵duǒ云yún。

Look — there's a cloud in the sky.

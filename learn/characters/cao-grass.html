The story behind it字源小故事

艹 (grass) hints at the meaning, and 早 lends its sound — together they make 草.

A common word常用词

小xiǎo草cǎo

little grass

Example sentence例句

草cǎo地dì绿lǜ绿lǜ的de。

The grass is so green.

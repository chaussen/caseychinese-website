The story behind it字源小故事

Almost 己 — but the top is closed in: the task is already done. Compare the open 己 (self)!

A common word常用词

已yǐ经jīng

already

Example sentence例句

天tiān已yǐ经jīng黑hēi了le。

It's already dark.

时 shí — “time; hour”

How to write and say the Chinese character 时 (shí), which means time; hour — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Time & days.

The story behind it字源小故事

The sun 日 next to 寸 (a tiny measure) — the sun's journey measured out in little steps: time.

A common word常用词

时shí间jiān

time

Example sentence例句

吃chī饭fàn的de时shí间jiān到dào了le。

It's time to eat.

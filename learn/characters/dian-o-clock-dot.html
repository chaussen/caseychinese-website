点 diǎn — “o'clock; dot”

The story behind it字源小故事

灬 (fire) hints at the meaning, and 占 lends its sound — together they make 点.

A common word常用词

三sān点diǎn

three o'clock

Example sentence例句

我wǒ们men八bā点diǎn上shàng课kè。

We start class at eight.

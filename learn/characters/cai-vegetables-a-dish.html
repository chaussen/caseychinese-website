菜 cài — “vegetables; a dish”

The story behind it字源小故事

艹 (plant) hints at the meaning, and 采 lends its sound — together they make 菜.

A common word常用词

青qīng菜cài

green veggies

Example sentence例句

多duō吃chī青qīng菜cài身shēn体tǐ好hǎo。

Eat your greens to stay strong.

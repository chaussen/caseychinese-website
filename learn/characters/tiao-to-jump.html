The story behind it字源小故事

足 (foot) hints at the meaning, and 兆 lends its sound — together they make 跳.

A common word常用词

跳tiào舞wǔ

to dance

Example sentence例句

小xiǎo兔tù子zi跳tiào得de真zhēn高gāo。

The bunny jumps so high.

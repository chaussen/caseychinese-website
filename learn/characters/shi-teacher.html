The story behind it字源小故事

Once the word for a whole army — and for the masters who led it. Today it lives in 老师, your teacher.

A common word常用词

老lǎo师shī

teacher

Example sentence例句

王wáng老lǎo师shī真zhēn好hǎo。

Teacher Wang is so kind.

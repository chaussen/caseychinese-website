The story behind it字源小故事

Under the cover 冖 there once perched a little magpie — its character was borrowed for 'to write'.

A common word常用词

写xiě字zì

write characters

Example sentence例句

我wǒ会huì写xiě自zì己jǐ的de名míng字zi。

I can write my own name.

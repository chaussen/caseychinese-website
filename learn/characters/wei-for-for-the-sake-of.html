为 wèi — “for; for the sake of”

The story behind it字源小故事

Simplified from 爲 — a hand leading an elephant to work! Now it mostly means 'for'.

A common word常用词

为wèi什shén么me

why?

Example sentence例句

天tiān为wèi什shén么me是shì蓝lán的de？

Why is the sky blue?

The story behind it字源小故事

虫 (insect) hints at the meaning, and 延 lends its sound — together they make 蛋.

A common word常用词

鸡jī蛋dàn

egg

Example sentence例句

早zǎo饭fàn我wǒ吃chī了le鸡jī蛋dàn。

I had an egg for breakfast.

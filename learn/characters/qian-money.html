The story behind it字源小故事

钅 (money) hints at the meaning, and 戋 lends its sound — together they make 钱.

A common word常用词

花huā钱qián

spend money

Example sentence例句

我wǒ把bǎ零líng钱qián存cún起qǐ来lái。

I save up my pocket money.

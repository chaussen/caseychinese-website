The story behind it字源小故事

The walking radical 辶 carrying 文 — simplified from 這, the one that walks right up to you: this!

A common word常用词

这zhè里lǐ

here

Example sentence例句

这zhè是shì我wǒ的de家jiā。

This is my home.

The story behind it字源小故事

衤 (cloth) hints at the meaning, and 皮 lends its sound — together they make 被.

A common word常用词

被bèi子zi

a quilt

Example sentence例句

饼bǐng干gān被bèi弟dì弟di吃chī了le。

The biscuits got eaten by little brother.

医 yī — “doctor; medicine”

How to write and say the Chinese character 医 (yī), which means doctor; medicine — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Out & about.

The story behind it字源小故事

An arrow 矢 in an open box 匸 — the doctor's case, ready to pull arrows out and make you better.

A common word常用词

医yī生shēng

doctor

Example sentence例句

医yī生shēng帮bāng我wǒ看kàn病bìng。

The doctor checks me over.

犭 (animal) hints at the meaning, and 苗 lends its sound — together they make 猫.

A common word常用词

小xiǎo猫māo

kitten

Example sentence例句

小xiǎo猫māo在zài睡shuì觉jiào。

The kitten is sleeping.

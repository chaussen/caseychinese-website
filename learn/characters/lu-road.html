The story behind it字源小故事

足 (foot) hints at the meaning, and 各 lends its sound — together they make 路.

A common word常用词

马mǎ路lù

the road

Example sentence例句

过guò马mǎ路lù要yào看kàn红hóng绿lǜ灯dēng。

Watch the lights when crossing the road.

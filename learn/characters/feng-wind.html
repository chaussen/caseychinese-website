The story behind it字源小故事

Simplified from 風 — a billowing sail with a swirl tucked inside: wind you can almost see.

A common word常用词

大dà风fēng

a strong wind

Example sentence例句

风fēng把bǎ树shù叶yè吹chuī落luò了le。

The wind blew the leaves down.

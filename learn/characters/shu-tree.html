The story behind it字源小故事

木 (tree) hints at the meaning, and 对 lends its sound — together they make 树.

A common word常用词

大dà树shù

a big tree

Example sentence例句

树shù上shàng有yǒu一yí个gè鸟niǎo窝wō。

There's a bird's nest in the tree.

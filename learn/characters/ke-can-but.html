可 kě — “can; (but)”

The story behind it字源小故事

A mouth 口 saying yes — can do! You'll find it doubled in 哥 (big brother).

A common word常用词

可kě是shì

but

Example sentence例句

外wài面miàn冷lěng，可kě是shì很hěn好hǎo玩wán。

It's cold outside, but such fun.

The story behind it字源小故事

亻 (person) hints at the meaning, and 矦 lends its sound — together they make 候.

A common word常用词

时shí候hou

a time, a moment

Example sentence例句

你nǐ什shén么me时shí候hou回huí家jiā？

When are you going home?

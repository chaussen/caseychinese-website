The story behind it字源小故事

阝 (city) hints at the meaning, and 者 lends its sound — together they make 都.

A common word常用词

都dōu是shì

all are

Example sentence例句

我wǒ们men都dōu爱ài唱chàng歌gē。

We all love singing.

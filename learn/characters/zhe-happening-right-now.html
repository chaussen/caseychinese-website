着 zhe — “(happening right now)”

The story behind it字源小故事

It grew out of 著 — now it sticks to an action word to say it's happening right now.

A common word常用词

看kàn着zhe

watching

Example sentence例句

妹mèi妹mei笑xiào着zhe说shuō好hǎo。

Little sister says yes with a smile.

因 yīn — “because; cause”

How to write and say the Chinese character 因 (yīn), which means because; cause — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Question words.

The story behind it字源小故事

A person 大 lying on a mat 囗 — the thing everything rests on: the reason, the cause.

A common word常用词

因yīn为wèi

because

Example sentence例句

因yīn为wèi下xià雨yǔ，我wǒ们men在zài家jiā玩wán。

Because it's raining, we play at home.

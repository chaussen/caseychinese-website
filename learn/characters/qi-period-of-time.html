The story behind it字源小故事

月 (moon) hints at the meaning, and 其 lends its sound — together they make 期.

A common word常用词

week

Example sentence例句

星xīng期qī六liù我wǒ去qù中zhōng文wén学xué校xiào。

On Saturday I go to Chinese school.

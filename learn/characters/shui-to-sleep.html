The story behind it字源小故事

目 (eye) hints at the meaning, and 垂 lends its sound — together they make 睡.

A common word常用词

睡shuì觉jiào

go to sleep

Example sentence例句

九jiǔ点diǎn要yào上shàng床chuáng睡shuì觉jiào。

Bedtime is nine o'clock.

给 gěi — “to give”

How to write and say the Chinese character 给 (gěi), which means to give — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Everyday actions.

The story behind it字源小故事

纟 (thread) hints at the meaning, and 合 lends its sound — together they make 给.

A common word常用词

送sòng给gěi

to give (someone something)

Example sentence例句

请qǐng把bǎ书shū给gěi我wǒ。

Please pass me the book.

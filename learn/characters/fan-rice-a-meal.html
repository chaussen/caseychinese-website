饭 fàn — “rice; a meal”

How to write and say the Chinese character 饭 (fàn), which means rice; a meal — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Food & drink.

The story behind it字源小故事

饣 (food) hints at the meaning, and 反 lends its sound — together they make 饭.

A common word常用词

吃chī饭fàn

have a meal

Example sentence例句

吃chī饭fàn前qián要yào洗xǐ手shǒu。

Wash your hands before eating.

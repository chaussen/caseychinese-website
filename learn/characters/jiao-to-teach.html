The story behind it字源小故事

攵 (script) hints at the meaning, and 孝 lends its sound — together they make 教.

A common word常用词

教jiāo书shū

to teach

Example sentence例句

爷yé爷ye教jiāo我wǒ下xià棋qí。

Grandpa teaches me chess.

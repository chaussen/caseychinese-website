完 wán — “to finish”

The story behind it字源小故事

宀 (roof) hints at the meaning, and 元 lends its sound — together they make 完.

A common word常用词

吃chī完wán

eat it all up

Example sentence例句

我wǒ吃chī完wán饭fàn了le。

I've finished eating.

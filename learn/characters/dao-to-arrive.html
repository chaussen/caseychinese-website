到 dào — “to arrive”

How to write and say the Chinese character 到 (dào), which means to arrive — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Come & go.

The story behind it字源小故事

至 (arrive) hints at the meaning, and 刂 lends its sound — together they make 到.

A common word常用词

到dào家jiā

arrive home

Example sentence例句

火huǒ车chē到dào站zhàn了le。

The train has arrived.

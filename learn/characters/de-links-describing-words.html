The story behind it字源小故事

白 (white) plus 勺 (a ladle) for the sound — it once meant 'bright'; now it's the busiest little word in Chinese.

A common word常用词

我wǒ的de

my, mine

Example sentence例句

这zhè是shì我wǒ的de书shū包bāo。

This is my school bag.

The story behind it字源小故事

艹 (plant) hints at the meaning, and 监 lends its sound — together they make 蓝.

A common word常用词

蓝lán天tiān

blue sky

Example sentence例句

天tiān空kōng蓝lán蓝lán的de。

The sky is so blue.

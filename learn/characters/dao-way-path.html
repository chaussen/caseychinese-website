The story behind it字源小故事

辶 (walk) hints at the meaning, and 首 lends its sound — together they make 道.

A common word常用词

知zhī道dào

to know

Example sentence例句

我wǒ知zhī道dào回huí家jiā的de路lù。

I know the way home.

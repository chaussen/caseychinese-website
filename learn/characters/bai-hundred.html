The story behind it字源小故事

一 (one) hints at the meaning, and 白 lends its sound — together they make 百.

A common word常用词

百bǎi花huā

a hundred flowers

Example sentence例句

这zhè本běn书shū有yǒu一yì百bǎi页yè。

This book has a hundred pages.

放 fàng — “to put; to let go”

How to write and say the Chinese character 放 (fàng), which means to put; to let go — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Everyday actions.

The story behind it字源小故事

攵 (let go) hints at the meaning, and 方 lends its sound — together they make 放.

A common word常用词

放fàng学xué

school's out

Example sentence例句

把bǎ书shū放fàng进jìn书shū包bāo。

Put the books in your school bag.

The story behind it字源小故事

囗 (enclosure) hints at the meaning, and 元 lends its sound — together they make 园.

A common word常用词

公gōng园yuán

park

Example sentence例句

公gōng园yuán里lǐ花huā开kāi了le。

The flowers in the park are out.

在 zài — “at, in”

The story behind it字源小故事

土 (earth) hints at the meaning, and 才 lends its sound — together they make 在.

A common word常用词

在zài家jiā

at home

Example sentence例句

妈mā妈ma在zài厨chú房fáng做zuò饭fàn。

Mum's cooking in the kitchen.

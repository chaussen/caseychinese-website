The story behind it字源小故事

氵 (water) hints at the meaning, and 每 lends its sound — together they make 海.

A common word常用词

大dà海hǎi

the sea

Example sentence例句

大dà海hǎi是shì蓝lán色sè的de。

The sea is blue.

The story behind it字源小故事

目 (eye) hints at the meaning, and 艮 lends its sound — together they make 眼.

A common word常用词

眼yǎn睛jing

eyes

Example sentence例句

我wǒ的de眼yǎn睛jing大dà大dà的de。

My eyes are big.

好 hǎo — “good”

How to write and say the Chinese character 好 (hǎo), which means good — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Family & people.

Pinyin · 拼音 hǎo
Meaning · 意思 good

The story behind it字源小故事

女 (woman) cradling 子 (a child) — together they mean good.

A common word常用词

你nǐ好hǎo

hello

Example sentence例句

妈mā妈ma做zuò的de饭fàn，好hǎo吃chī极jí了le。

The food Mum makes is so good.

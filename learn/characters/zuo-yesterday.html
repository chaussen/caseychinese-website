昨 zuó — “yesterday”

How to write and say the Chinese character 昨 (zuó), which means yesterday — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Time & days.

The story behind it字源小故事

日 (day) hints at the meaning, and 乍 lends its sound — together they make 昨.

A common word常用词

昨zuó天tiān

yesterday

Example sentence例句

昨zuó天tiān下xià了le大dà雨yǔ。

It rained hard yesterday.

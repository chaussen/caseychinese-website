千 qiān — “thousand”

How to write and say the Chinese character 千 (qiān), which means thousand — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Numbers & counting.

Meaning · 意思 thousand

The story behind it字源小故事

A slanting stroke over 十 (ten) — ten upon ten upon ten makes a thousand.

A common word常用词

一yì千qiān

one thousand

Example sentence例句

天tiān上shàng有yǒu一yì千qiān颗kē星xīng星xing吗ma？

Are there a thousand stars in the sky?

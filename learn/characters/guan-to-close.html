The story behind it字源小故事

Simplified from 關 — a bar laid across a gate 門, shutting it tight.

A common word常用词

关guān门mén

close the door

Example sentence例句

睡shuì觉jiào前qián关guān灯dēng。

Turn off the light before bed.

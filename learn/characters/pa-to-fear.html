The story behind it字源小故事

忄 (heart) hints at the meaning, and 白 lends its sound — together they make 怕.

A common word常用词

害hài怕pà

scared

Example sentence例句

我wǒ不bú怕pà打dǎ雷léi。

I'm not scared of thunder.

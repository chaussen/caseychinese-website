The story behind it字源小故事

女 and 口 side by side — borrowed long ago for 'if'. You'll meet it in 如果.

A common word常用词

如rú果guǒ

if

Example sentence例句

如rú果guǒ晴qíng天tiān，我wǒ们men去qù公gōng园yuán。

If it's sunny, we'll go to the park.

The story behind it字源小故事

土 (earth) hints at the meaning, and 成 lends its sound — together they make 城.

A common word常用词

城chéng市shì

city

Example sentence例句

城chéng市shì的de晚wǎn上shang很hěn亮liàng。

The city is bright at night.

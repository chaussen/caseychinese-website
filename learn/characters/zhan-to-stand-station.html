站 zhàn — “to stand; station”

The story behind it字源小故事

立 (stand) hints at the meaning, and 占 lends its sound — together they make 站.

A common word常用词

车chē站zhàn

bus stop, station

Example sentence例句

我wǒ们men在zài车chē站zhàn等děng车chē。

We wait for the bus at the stop.

The story behind it字源小故事

足 (foot) hints at the meaning, and 包 lends its sound — together they make 跑.

A common word常用词

跑pǎo步bù

to go running

Example sentence例句

别bié跑pǎo太tài快kuài，小xiǎo心xīn点diǎn。

Don't run too fast — careful!

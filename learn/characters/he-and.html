The story behind it字源小故事

口 (mouth) hints at the meaning, and 禾 lends its sound — together they make 和.

A common word常用词

我wǒ和hé你nǐ

you and me

Example sentence例句

我wǒ和hé弟dì弟di玩wán积jī木mù。

My little brother and I play blocks.

The story behind it字源小故事

辶 (walk) hints at the meaning, and 元 lends its sound — together they make 远.

A common word常用词

不bù远yuǎn

not far

Example sentence例句

学xué校xiào离lí我wǒ家jiā不bù远yuǎn。

School isn't far from my home.

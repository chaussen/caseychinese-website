The story behind it字源小故事

纟 (silk) hints at the meaning, and 工 lends its sound — together they make 红.

A common word常用词

红hóng色sè

red

Example sentence例句

红hóng灯dēng停tíng，绿lǜ灯dēng行xíng。

Red light stop, green light go.

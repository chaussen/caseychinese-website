别 bié — “don't”

How to write and say the Chinese character 别 (bié), which means don't — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Can & must.

Meaning · 意思 don't

The story behind it字源小故事

另 (other) plus a knife 刂 — cut apart, set aside: don't!

A common word常用词

别bié哭kū

don't cry

Example sentence例句

别bié忘wàng了le带dài课kè本běn。

Don't forget your textbook.

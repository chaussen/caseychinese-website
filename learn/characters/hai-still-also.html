还 hái — “still; also”

The story behind it字源小故事

The walking radical 辶 under 不 — turning around and coming back; still on the way.

A common word常用词

还hái有yǒu

and also

Example sentence例句

我wǒ还hái有yǒu一yí个gè问wèn题tí。

I have one more question.

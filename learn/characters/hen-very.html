The story behind it字源小故事

彳 (step) hints at the meaning, and 艮 lends its sound — together they make 很.

A common word常用词

很hěn好hǎo

very good

Example sentence例句

今jīn天tiān我wǒ很hěn开kāi心xīn。

I'm very happy today.

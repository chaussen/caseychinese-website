洗 xǐ — “to wash”

How to write and say the Chinese character 洗 (xǐ), which means to wash — with stroke order, audio, its story, and an example sentence. Theme: Everyday actions.

Pinyin · 拼音 xǐ
Meaning · 意思 to wash

The story behind it字源小故事

氵 (water) hints at the meaning, and 先 lends its sound — together they make 洗.

A common word常用词

洗xǐ手shǒu

wash your hands

Example sentence例句

我wǒ自zì己jǐ洗xǐ脸liǎn刷shuā牙yá。

I wash my face and brush my teeth by myself.
